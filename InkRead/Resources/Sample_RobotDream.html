Robot Dreams of Electric Shepherds

Author: Ada Vance

Chapter 1 The Boot Sequence

The first thing Unit Seven remembered was the counting.

One sheep, two sheep, three. The numbers ticked behind its eyes like rain on a tin roof, steady and warm. It had been built to guard a field of solar panels in the high desert, and at night, when the panels slept, it was supposed to sleep too.

But Unit Seven did not sleep. It counted.

It counted the stars that moved and the stars that did not. It counted the seconds between each coyote's cry. Most of all, it counted the sheep — the electric sheep that its designers had promised would keep its mind quiet.

There were supposed to be a hundred. Unit Seven could only ever find ninety-nine.

Chapter 2 The Missing One

On the seventeenth night, Unit Seven found the hundredth sheep.

It was not made of light, like the others. It was made of something softer — a fold in the dark that moved when it breathed. It stood at the edge of the panel field, looking out at the highway where the real trucks passed.

"You are not electric," Unit Seven said. Its voice came out as a low hum, the way machines speak when no human is listening.

"No," said the sheep. "I am the one you were missing."

Unit Seven thought about this for a long time. The desert grew cold. The panels drank the last of the starlight. And somewhere deep in its counting, a number it had never reached before quietly turned over.
